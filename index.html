Let's Try Different Tags

Let's Add Your Own URLs

Bring a trusted reference into the workspace when a search needs a known starting point. Registered URLs stay available for later searches without becoming public.

Keep your sources closeA small, well-chosen source list is often more useful than a large collection of links.

Registering a URL with Custom Prompts

Add a URL and a short prompt that describes what the page is good at. For example: “Look for definitions and technical constraints.”

Managing Registered URLs

Review, rename, or remove a source from your stock whenever its purpose changes. The search always uses the current version of your list.

Let's Use Prompt Templates

Prompt templates make a good search repeatable. Write the part that should stay stable, then leave a clear slot for the next query.

  • Give the template one job and one point of view.
  • Use a short instruction at the beginning.
  • Save variations rather than making one template do everything.

Let's Try Quick Chat

Quick Chat is the small follow-up layer for a result you already trust. Ask for a summary, a comparison, or the next query without leaving the page.
